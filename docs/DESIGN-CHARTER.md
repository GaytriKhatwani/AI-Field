# AI Field — Design Charter (Direction: The Standing Assignment)

> The committed product/design direction: **C — The Standing Assignment**, raised by **B** (the build-forward / examine-back evidence line, concentrated in the Debrief) and **A** (context as a deliberate act the user performs). Governs all design work. No fonts/colors committed yet — those get chosen when we build. Supersedes the exploration in `DESIGN-DIRECTIONS.md`; grounded in `EXPERIENCE-THESIS.md` and screened against `ANTI-PATTERNS.md`.

## Conceptual backbone

**Do the work → leave an evidence trail → receive an honest read → the gap generates the next assignment.**

Everything below serves this one loop.

## Two tensions that drive the composition

1. **ONE NEXT REP** ⟷ **A LARGER PRACTICE WORLD EXISTS.** Resolve by *hierarchy*, not by adding a browse surface: the next rep is singular and present; the wider world (other missions, completed lineage) is legible but quiet and subordinate — never a grid or catalogue.
2. **CAPABILITY IS IMPORTANT** ⟷ **DON'T TURN CAPABILITY INTO ANALYTICS.** Resolve by representing capability as *glanceable states of practice / evidence of what you've shown* — never as a chart, meter, or panel.

## Principles

Each is specific enough to reject a design.

### One rep, inside a larger practice
1. **The recommended next rep is the center of gravity** — always the single most present thing, earning that presence through focus and position, *never* through a large "featured card."
2. **The larger world is legible but subordinate.** The Field must let the user understand, without becoming a browse surface: their recommended mission (primary), that other missions exist and are reachable (secondary — discoverable, not a course catalogue), and what they've completed (a lineage/record, not a lesson list). *Reject any Field that reads as a dashboard, catalogue, or LMS home.*
3. **Entering a mission clears the world.** Accepting a brief recedes the Field and opens a focused working mode; the surroundings don't persist as chrome. *Reject persistent sidebars/nav inside the work.*
4. **The experience is continuing practice, not browsing content.** Every surface should feel like the next step in an ongoing practice.

### Capability — legible, not analytical
5. **The five capabilities are readable at a glance as states of practice.** Context, Direction, Iteration, Verification, Synthesis — with which are developed vs. still largely unexplored obvious instantly, and *without* a radar chart, five giant progress bars, percentages, or an analytics panel. *Reject any capability display that reads as a stats widget.*
6. **Capability is evidence, not a scoreboard.** Unexplored capabilities read as genuinely open (not "0%"), and that openness is part of what pulls the next rep. The Operator Profile is a retention mechanism *because* it shows an honest, incomplete picture — never because it gamifies one. *Reject numeric precision and vanity framing.*

### The evidence line (from B, bounded)
7. **Build the session forward, examine the same session afterward.** The attempt the user builds is the exact artifact the examiner reads back. The evidence/session line is a real part of the interaction language, **concentrated in the Debrief.**
8. **Evaluation is anchored to actual moments,** never detached feedback — coaching points to what the user did and when.
9. **The line serves evidence and reflection only.** *Reject it wherever it would spread into a product-wide timeline, activity feed, analytics view, or progress tracker.*

### Context as a deliberate act (from A, simplified)
10. **Resources are visibly outside the AI's context until the user gives them.** Recognizing required context is a measured competency, so the act must be the user's and must be visible.
11. **The give-context interaction is simple, not a canvas:** *resource available → a deliberate "give to the AI" act → visible, persistent confirmation the AI now has that context.* No drag-and-drop or spatial canvas required for the MVP. *Reject any design where context is auto-injected, ambient, or provided without a clear user act and clear confirmation.*

### The Workbench stance
12. **The Workbench is organized around producing the deliverable; the AI is an instrument subordinate to that outcome, not the main product.** *Reject any layout where the chat is the hero and the deliverable is a side panel — that is the ChatGPT-wrapper failure.*

### The loop resolves forward
13. **Completing a mission resolves into receiving the next rep** — the gap named in the honest read *becomes* the next assignment; finishing and being handed the next are one motion. *Reject a terminal "done" screen with a menu; the practice continues.*

### Tone / voice guardrails
14. **The "demanding mentor / apprenticeship" idea lives at the level of product structure only** — one real job at a time, watched, returned with honest judgment. **No** mentor avatars, **no** mentor chat, **no** teacher or classroom language, **no** congratulatory coaching clichés, **no** patronizing encouragement. Treat the user as a capable working professional owed the truth. *Reject any copy or character that personifies a coach or talks down.*
