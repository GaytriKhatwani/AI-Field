# The Field — Three Structural Compositions (IA / hierarchy / spatial study)

> Round scope: **The Field only.** Information architecture, hierarchy, and spatial composition — no Briefing/Workbench/Debrief, no code, no final fonts/colors/shadows. Governed by `DESIGN-CHARTER.md`; screened against `ANTI-PATTERNS.md`.
>
> The Field must let the user answer in ~5 seconds: (1) what to practise next, (2) why it's the right rep, (3) which capabilities they're developing, (4) what else exists, (5) whether they're building an ongoing body of capability — and feel *"this is the next piece of work I should take on,"* not *"here is my learning dashboard."*
>
> All three models pass the container-discipline test with **zero cards**: grouping is done by proximity, scale, dimming, rules, and spatial position. All three explicitly reject the banned stack `[header][big rec card][profile card][more-missions grid]`.

The three differ in **how information is organized**, not where boxes sit:

- **A — Foreground Assignment / Peripheral World** → organized by **depth & priority** (one thing forward, everything else receded).
- **B — Capability Spine** → organized by **the five capabilities** (the taxonomy is the page's skeleton; work hangs off it).
- **C — The Practitioner's Ledger** → organized by **record & progression** (a running index of work done and to do; the next rep is its open head).

---

## Model A — Foreground Assignment / Peripheral World

Figure-ground. The next rep is the **figure**, stated at full scale in the optical center. The identity, the capability register, and the wider practice world are the **ground** — pushed to the margins and dimmed. Nothing is a module stacked below; the world literally sits *around and behind* the one piece of work.

### 1. Structural sketch
```
  AI FIELD                                   CONTEXT   ·  DIRECTION
                                             ITERATION ·  VERIFICATION
                                             SYNTHESIS
                                          (five words, each carrying its own
                                           state by weight: solid / medium /
                                           faint / ghost-outline)

            your next rep — because Verification is still unexplored

            MEETING  CHAOS
            Turn a mess of meeting notes into decisions and owners.
            ~10 min  ·  builds Context · Direction · Synthesis

                       Begin  →

  · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · ·
   also in your practice   the bad prompt    the brief    don't trust the ai (not yet)
   your worked record gathers along this edge as you finish reps
```
(The lower line is faint inline text at the margin — not tiles, not a grid, not a carousel.)

### 2. Attention sequence
1st → **MEETING CHAOS** (largest type, optical center).
2nd → the *why* line + the one-line premise directly above/below it.
3rd → **Begin →**.
4th → only when the user goes looking: the peripheral capability register (top-right) and the faint world of other work (bottom margin).

### 3. Recommended mission — dominant without a card
Dominance comes from **scale, isolation, and centering**, not a container. The title is the largest thing on the surface, sits in the optical center with air around it, and is the *only* element rendered at full contrast — everything else is dimmed to ground. There is no border, no fill, no "featured" chrome; it dominates because it's the one thing in focus and everything else has stepped back.

### 4. Capability — glanceable, concrete mechanism
Top-right, the five capability **words** are always present, each rendered in one of four type-states: **solid = demonstrated · medium = developing · faint = lightly demonstrated · ghost/outline = unexplored.** State is read from the *presence of the word itself* — no bar, no number, no chart. The capability being targeted also appears by name in the rep's *why* line ("because Verification is still unexplored"), tying the recommendation to the gap. Inspecting a word reveals the moments behind its state (the evidence line).

### 5. Wider practice world — discoverable, not a catalogue
The other missions live as **faint inline text along the lower margin** — legible as "there's more here" but visually subordinate. They're discoverable by looking to the edge or hovering, and expand in place on inspect. Not-yet-relevant ones are set even further back with a plain reason on inspect. No grid, no carousel, no "More missions" heading over tiles.

### 6. First-time state
The figure (Meeting Chaos) is full and strong, so the screen never reads as empty. The capability register shows **all five in ghost/unexplored** — an honest "nothing shown yet" that becomes the pull. The lower margin names the three other missions faintly plus "your worked record gathers along this edge" as intentional potential, not a broken empty state.

### 7. Returning state
Same composition; the ground fills in. The capability register now shows mixed states; the *why* line references recent evidence; the figure is the gap-targeted rep. Completed reps accumulate as faint entries along the lower-margin record — the periphery thickens over time without ever becoming the subject.

### 8. Interaction
- **Begin recommended:** primary action on the figure; entering clears the world into the focused work mode (per charter).
- **Inspect another mission:** the faint margin name expands in place to premise + effort + what it builds — a peek, never a jump to a catalogue.
- **Inspect capability:** the word expands to the moments/evidence behind its state.
- **Completed mission:** joins the lower-margin record as a faint entry and updates the relevant capability word's state.
- **Locked / not-yet-relevant:** rendered most-receded, with a plain reason on inspect ("opens after a full brief") — no padlock, no badge.

### 9. Why this is AI Field
Remove the words and it still reads as *one piece of work held in focus with an entire practice dimmed behind it.* No learning platform shows a single dominant assignment with the whole world receded to margins; that singular figure-ground focus is the product's stance made visible.

### 10. Failure mode
Collapses to a dashboard if the periphery hardens into a tile row/carousel, or if the capability register becomes five aligned bars. Reads as broken if "dimmed" looks "disabled." If the *why* line is dropped, it becomes a generic hero.

## Model B — Capability Spine

The five capabilities are the **structure of the page** — a standing column that always exists. Work isn't listed separately; it hangs off the capability it builds. The recommended rep is the **one activated line**: the gap capability opens inline to reveal the next rep.

### 1. Structural sketch
```
  AI FIELD                                                 your practice

  CONTEXT          developing        · from 2 reps
  DIRECTION        developing        · from 2 reps
  ITERATION        lightly shown     · from 1 rep
  VERIFICATION     unexplored   ←— your gap, and your next rep:

                     DON'T TRUST THE AI
                     Catch what the AI gets wrong.
                     ~10 min · also builds Direction        Begin →

  SYNTHESIS        demonstrated      · from 3 reps
```
(No card around the rep — it's the expanded body of the Verification line. State per capability is carried by the word + its label, not a bar.)

### 2. Attention sequence
1st → the five capability **words** as a column (the shape of the practice).
2nd → the one marked **your gap** (set apart by state + label).
3rd → the rep that opens beneath it + **Begin →**.
4th → the quiet "from N reps" evidence markers down the right.

### 3. Recommended mission — dominant without a card
The rep dominates **locally**: it's the only capability line that expands, the only place with body text and an action, indented into focus while the other four stay as single quiet lines. Dominance by *activation and expansion within the spine*, not a container. (Trade-off: capability is read fractionally before the rep — see failure mode.)

### 4. Capability — glanceable, concrete mechanism
Capability isn't a module — it **is** the page. Each of the five lines carries its state in the word's treatment plus a one-word label (demonstrated / developing / lightly shown / unexplored) and a faint "from N reps" evidence marker. Four states, glanceable, no bars/numbers/chart. Inspecting a line expands the reps and moments that produced that state.

### 5. Wider practice world — discoverable, not a catalogue
Other missions are discovered **through capabilities**: inspecting any capability reveals the reps that build it (done + available). Discovery is framed as "what builds this skill," so the practice world is organized by consequence, not shelved as a catalogue. (A mission that builds several capabilities appears under each.)

### 6. First-time state
Strongest first-run of the three: the spine exists independently of any data, so the page is **never empty** — all five show **unexplored**, an honest full picture on day one. Caveat: with no evidence there's no "gap" yet, so the first rep attaches to **Context** framed as "start here" rather than as a gap.

### 7. Returning state
The lines diverge into varied states; the weakest becomes the marked gap with the rep attached; "from N reps" grows; completed work is reachable by inspecting any capability. The page visibly *is* the growing shape of your ability.

### 8. Interaction
- **Begin recommended:** action on the expanded gap line; clears into work mode.
- **Inspect another mission:** reached via the capability it builds; expands in place.
- **Inspect capability:** the line opens to its evidence and the reps under it.
- **Completed mission:** strengthens its capabilities' states and their evidence counts.
- **Locked / not-yet-relevant:** appears under its capability, receded, with a plain reason — no padlock.

### 9. Why this is AI Field
Remove the words and it still reads as *a practice defined by five named capabilities, with your standing in each shown as the shape of the words themselves, and the next work emerging from your weakest.* That "the skills are the page and the gap produces the work" structure is unmistakably this product's thesis, not a generic home.

### 10. Failure mode
Highest risk of the three. Becomes the banned dashboard the instant the capability lines read as five progress bars; becomes an LMS competency framework/curriculum checklist if the language turns academic; and it structurally *over-elevates capability*, which can bury the "one next rep" the charter says must be the center of gravity.

## Model C — The Practitioner's Ledger

Organized as a **running record of practice**. The next rep is the *open head* of the ledger; completed work accumulates below as the body; forthcoming work waits beneath that. One continuous column, structured by rules and three quiet registers (OPEN / YOUR RECORD / NOT YET) — no cards, no sections-of-tiles.

### 1. Structural sketch
```
  AI FIELD — your practice record        Context  Direction  Iteration
                                          Verification  Synthesis
                                          (state line: each word weighted by
                                           how far you've taken it)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  OPEN
    →  DON'T TRUST THE AI                                        ~10 min
       Recommended — your Verification is still unexplored.
       Catch what the AI gets wrong.                     Begin →
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  YOUR RECORD
    ·  The Brief          showed Direction, Synthesis
    ·  Meeting Chaos      showed Context; Verification thin
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  NOT YET
       The Bad Prompt — opens once you've completed a full brief
```

### 2. Attention sequence
1st → the **OPEN** head entry title (the next rep, largest).
2nd → its "recommended because…" + **Begin →**.
3rd → the masthead **state line** (capabilities).
4th → **YOUR RECORD** accumulating below, then **NOT YET**.

### 3. Recommended mission — dominant without a card
Dominance by **editorial lead position + scale + singularity**: it's the sole entry under OPEN, set in the largest type, the only line carrying an action, sitting at the head of the ledger. Every other entry is a lighter record line. It leads the way a lead story leads a page — no container needed.

### 4. Capability — glanceable, concrete mechanism
A compact **state line** in the masthead: the five capability words, each weighted/marked by state (four states) — read like a status line, not a chart. The **RECORD** below is the *evidence* behind that line: each completed entry notes which capabilities it showed. Inspecting a word in the state line highlights the record entries that built it — glance in the masthead, proof in the record.

### 5. Wider practice world — discoverable, not a catalogue
Other work reads as **entries in the same ledger** — under OPEN/NOT YET now, and as the record's future lines. The whole practice is legible as "the shape of a record you're keeping," not a shelf of courses. NOT YET entries carry a plain reason inline (no padlock).

### 6. First-time state
The RECORD register is empty, so it's replaced by a single honest line — *"Your record begins with this rep"* — rather than a blank block. The OPEN lead (Meeting Chaos) carries the page; the state line shows all five unexplored. Thinnest first-run of the three, but the ledger framing makes emptiness read as "day one," not "broken."

### 7. Returning state
The RECORD becomes the substance of the page — the accumulating body answers "am I building capability?" better than any other model. The OPEN head updates to the gap rep; the state line updates; NOT YET shrinks as work unlocks.

### 8. Interaction
- **Begin recommended:** action on the OPEN head; clears into work mode.
- **Inspect another mission:** a NOT YET / future entry expands to premise + effort + what it builds, in place.
- **Inspect capability:** a state-line word highlights/filters the record entries that evidence it.
- **Completed mission:** drops into YOUR RECORD as a new entry and updates the state line.
- **Locked / not-yet-relevant:** a NOT YET entry with its plain unlock reason — no padlock, no badge.

### 9. Why this is AI Field
Remove the words and it still reads as *a working professional's own record of real reps, led by the next open assignment.* It's a record you keep, not a course you consume — the accumulating body of real work is the identity.

### 10. Failure mode
Collapses to an **activity feed** if the record becomes timestamped events, or a **course catalogue** if NOT YET grows into a long browse-list, or **analytics** if the state line acquires counts/percentages. The lead loses its force if OPEN ever holds more than one entry.

## Comparison

Scored 1–5 (5 = strongest).

| Axis | A · Foreground | B · Capability Spine | C · Ledger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity of next action | **5** | 3½ | 4½ |
| Visibility of capability | 3½ | **5** | 4 |
| Discoverability of broader practice | 3½ | 4 | **4½** |
| Distinctiveness | 4½ | **5** | 4 |
| Usability (non-technical) | **5** | 3½ | 4½ |
| First-time-user quality | 4 | **4½** | 3½ |
| Scalability as missions grow | 3½ | 4 | **4½** |
| MVP implementation feasibility | **4½** | 4 | **4½** |

**Reading it:** the totals are close. **B** wins capability visibility and distinctiveness but structurally over-elevates capability — its own failure mode is the exact dashboard/LMS risk, and reading capability *before* the next rep works against the 5-second priority and the charter's "rep is the center of gravity." **C** best answers "am I building a body of capability?" and scales best, but has the weakest first-run and sits nearest a known list pattern. **A** most directly delivers the emotional target — *"this is the next piece of work"* — keeps the rep unambiguously dominant with capability and world honestly subordinate, and is the most usable for non-technical professionals and among the most feasible.

## Recommendation

**Model A — Foreground Assignment / Peripheral World**, raised by two borrowed mechanisms:

1. **B's four-state typographic capability register** — so capability is glanceable-as-states, never analytics.
2. **C's accumulating record** — so the periphery *becomes a growing body of worked reps* over time, answering "am I building capability?" and scaling as missions increase.

This keeps the recommended rep as the singular figure the charter demands, while giving capability and the wider practice world real, non-dashboard presence. A's one danger — a lazy execution that hardens the periphery into tiles/bars and reverts to the banned stack — is a discipline problem to hold the line on in the next round, not a structural flaw.

Recommended, not committed, and no visual styling chosen. Next round would test this composition further (still The Field) before any styling.
