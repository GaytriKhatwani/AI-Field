# The Field — Model A Stress-Test (four states) + revised model

> Scope: The Field only. IA / hierarchy / spatial composition. No code, no final fonts/colors/shadows/decoration, no other screens. Governed by `DESIGN-CHARTER.md`; builds on `THE-FIELD-COMPOSITIONS.md` (Model A chosen). This document supersedes the raw Model A sketch for The Field.

## System-level resolutions (apply to every state)

**Container discipline.** The default Field is uncontained; the recommended rep dominates by scale, space, position, and contrast — never a card. A container earns its keep in exactly one place: a **transient inspection layer** (peeking a mission, a capability, or the record), where a bounded, dismissable surface is the correct affordance for "focused detail you can close."

**Capability state language (robust, not opacity).** Three redundant channels, none of them a meter:
- **Marker (categorical, by shape):** `○` = not yet worked · `●` = worked.
- **Evidence-phrase label (the four states, in words, always full-contrast):** *not yet worked · shown once · shown in a few reps · shown consistently.*
- **Weight (reinforcement only):** more-demonstrated reads heavier — but the label is primary, so weight never carries state alone.
- The **gap** is annotated in words (`← your gap`) and mirrored in the figure's *why* clause. Unexplored is rendered at full legibility (open marker + plain label), reading as "open/ahead," never disabled. No opacity-only, color-only, or hover-only state anywhere.

**Persistent skeleton (same regions in all four states — one system evolving, not four layouts):**
- `[identity]` small, top.
- `[figure]` the recommended rep — the only element at display scale: *why/gap clause · title · one-line premise · ~effort · builds X·Y · Begin →*.
- `[capability register]` the fixed five, same order, using the state language above.
- `[practice world]` a **bounded** few next-relevant missions as legible, obviously-interactive entries + a `see all missions ›` opener; not-yet-relevant items carry a plain reason (no padlock).
- `[record]` where completed reps accumulate — shown as a **compact summary** by default (count + recent few, no timestamps); full history one layer down via `your practice ›`.

**Scaling rule (load-bearing):** the default Field always renders a *bounded slice* — 5 capabilities + ~3 missions + 1 record summary — regardless of totals. Everything unbounded (full mission list, full history, per-capability evidence) lives one inspection layer down.

## STATE 1 — First visit (0 completed, 0 evidence)

```
 AI FIELD                                                 your practice ›
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                     start here

                     MEETING  CHAOS
                     Turn a mess of meeting notes into decisions and owners.
                     ~10 min · builds Context · Direction · Synthesis
                              Begin →

     the rest of your practice
       →  The Bad Prompt        rewrite a weak prompt into a strong one
       →  The Brief             turn a vague ask into a usable brief
       ·  Don't Trust the AI    opens once you've worked a full brief
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CONTEXT ○ not yet worked   DIRECTION ○ not yet worked   ITERATION ○ not yet worked
  VERIFICATION ○ not yet worked        SYNTHESIS ○ not yet worked
                    your record begins with this rep
```

- **Sparse, not empty:** the figure is fully substantial; the capability register is *fully populated* (five real capabilities, all honestly "not yet worked" — a complete picture of the practice ahead, not five blank slots); the practice world names real missions. The only true placeholder is one intentional record line.
- **Five unexplored without zeroed metrics:** state is "not yet worked" (a condition), never "0%/0 pts." There is no gauge to sit at zero; five identical `○` reads as "a whole practice ahead," not "five empty meters."
- **Attention:** 1st MEETING CHAOS → 2nd *start here* + premise + Begin → 3rd capability register (the horizon of five) → 4th the other missions / `your practice ›`.
- **Capability mechanism:** the fixed five-item register, each `○ not yet worked`. No gap marked yet (no evidence to compare).
- **Practice-world mechanism:** three named, legible, interactive entries; the not-yet one shows its plain reason inline.
- **Completed-work mechanism:** the record line "your record begins with this rep" — the seed of the accumulating body.
- **Why Meeting Chaos is obvious:** sole figure at display scale, "start here" (no gap logic yet), and the only Begin action.
- **Interaction:** *inspect capability* → layer explaining what it means + "first shown when you…"; *inspect another mission* → layer with premise/effort/builds/Begin (temporarily raises it); *inspect previous work* → nothing yet (record is the single seed line); *begin recommended* → clears into the work mode.

```
 AI FIELD                                                 your practice ›
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                     because your Verification is still unexplored

                     DON'T TRUST THE AI
                     Catch what the AI gets wrong before you rely on it.
                     ~10 min · builds Verification · Direction
                              Begin →

     the rest of your practice
       →  The Bad Prompt        rewrite a weak prompt into a strong one
       →  The Brief             turn a vague ask into a usable brief
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CONTEXT ● shown once   DIRECTION ● shown once   ITERATION ● shown once
  VERIFICATION ○ not yet worked ← your gap        SYNTHESIS ● shown in a few reps
             your practice — 1 rep · Meeting Chaos (showed Context)
```

- **Recommendation explains itself through capability:** the figure's clause *"because your Verification is still unexplored"* names the gap; the register's `VERIFICATION ○ not yet worked ← your gap` mirrors it. Gap → assignment is visible on the surface.
- **How Meeting Chaos enters:** it leaves the "available" list and appears in the record summary ("1 rep · Meeting Chaos (showed Context)"), and it has moved the capability states.
- **What changed from State 1:** capability states diverged (four now `●`, Verification marked as gap); the figure is now the gap rep, not "start here"; the record holds its first real entry.
- **Feels like theirs:** the ground now reflects *their* evidence — the states and the record are personal facts, not defaults.
- **Attention:** 1st DON'T TRUST THE AI → 2nd the *why*/gap + Begin → 3rd capability register (now varied, gap marked) → 4th record ("your first rep") / other missions.
- **Mechanisms:** capability = register with diverged states + gap annotation; practice-world = same bounded entries (the completed one now lives in the record); completed-work = the record summary begins to grow.
- **Interaction:** *inspect capability* → layer shows the moments behind the state (e.g., "Context — shown in Meeting Chaos when you attached the notes and set owners"); *inspect another mission* → peek layer; *inspect previous work* → peek the Meeting Chaos rep + its evidence; *begin recommended* → work mode.

## STATE 3 — Established learner (8–10 done, evidence across all five)

```
 AI FIELD                                                 your practice ›
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                     because your Iteration has only been shown once

                     THE REVISION PASS
                     Push a mediocre draft to something you'd actually send.
                     ~12 min · builds Iteration · Synthesis
                              Begin →

     the rest of your practice                            see all missions ›
       →  Signal & Noise        separate the real finding from the filler
       →  The Handoff           brief a colleague through the AI
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CONTEXT ● shown consistently   DIRECTION ● shown in a few reps   ITERATION ● shown once ← your gap
  VERIFICATION ● shown in a few reps        SYNTHESIS ● shown consistently
        your practice — 9 reps worked · recent: The Brief, Signal & Noise, The Bad Prompt   ›
```

- **Completed work accumulates without a feed:** the record is a **compact summary** — a count + a few recent names, framed as a body of practice, **no timestamps** and no scroll. Full history is one layer down (`›`). No time axis in the default view → it can't read as an activity feed.
- **Discovery scales without a catalogue:** the practice-world zone shows only the **2–3 next-relevant** missions; `see all missions ›` holds the rest. The Field never renders the full list, so it never becomes a catalogue.
- **Next rep stays dominant:** yes — still the only display-scale element, and because the ground is bounded (5 + ~3 + 1 summary) it cannot out-mass the figure no matter how many missions/attempts exist.
- **Capability stays glanceable:** the same fixed five-item register, now all populated and varied, gap marked. Five is constant → trivially scalable.
- **Default vs. on-inspection:** *default* = figure, capability register, ~3 next-relevant missions, one record summary line, openers. *On inspection* = full mission list, full history, per-capability evidence, per-mission detail.
- **Attention:** 1st the figure → 2nd why/gap + Begin → 3rd capability register → 4th record summary / next-relevant missions.
- **Completed-work mechanism:** compact summary (count + recent few, no timestamps) → `your practice ›` opens the full record layer (grouped by mission/capability, **not** by time — so even the full view isn't a feed).
- **Interaction:** *inspect capability* → layer with its evidence trail across reps (bounded, "+ earlier"); *inspect another mission* → peek; *inspect previous work* → opens the record layer (a **contained** surface — earns its place); *begin recommended* → work mode.

## STATE 4 — User explores instead of taking the recommendation

```
 AI FIELD                                                 your practice ›
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ┌ inspecting ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  THE HANDOFF                                                          │
  │  Brief a colleague through the AI so they can run with it.           │
  │  ~10 min · builds Direction · Synthesis                              │
  │                                     Begin this instead →             │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
     your recommendation is still The Revision Pass   ·   back to it →

     the rest of your practice                            see all missions ›
       →  Signal & Noise        separate the real finding from the filler
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  CONTEXT ● shown consistently   DIRECTION ● shown in a few reps   ITERATION ● shown once
  VERIFICATION ● shown in a few reps        SYNTHESIS ● shown consistently
```

- **Periphery → consideration:** tapping a mission in the practice-world zone raises it into a **focused inspection layer** (the contained surface that earns its keep) with premise, effort, the capabilities it builds, and *Begin this instead →*.
- **Temporary figure/ground swap:** yes — the inspected mission becomes the temporary figure; the recommendation recedes to ground but stays **named and one tap away** (`your recommendation is still The Revision Pass · back to it →`). It never animates or competes; it's just reliably present.
- **Recommendation stays identifiable without fighting:** it keeps its label and a persistent "back to it" line — no fanfare, no pulsing.
- **Return path:** *back to it →*, or dismissing the layer restores the default Field with the rep as figure.
- **Browse-all needed?** Not for the common case — discovery is **contextual** (inspect from the bounded list). For breadth there's a secondary `see all missions ›` view, never the default and never a prescribed path.
- **Capability in the choice:** the inspection layer shows which capabilities the mission builds, so the user can weigh it against their own gap — capability reasoning travels into the decision.
- **Attention (while inspecting):** 1st the inspected mission → 2nd *Begin this instead* → 3rd the persistent "recommendation · back to it" line → 4th the rest.
- **Interaction:** the four inspects behave as above; here the mission-inspect is elevated to a temporary figure rather than a small peek, because the user signaled intent to consider it.

## Impeccable stress critique

- **Singular focus — holds.** The figure is the only display-scale element and the ground is bounded, so it stays the center of gravity across all states. Watch item: the why/gap clause and register must never grow to rival the figure; the figure is always largest and owns the sole default action.
- **Discoverability — holds (was the failure point in naive A).** Bounded named entries + `see all` opener guarantee the user sees that more exists and can reach it. Raw Model A (faint peripheral text) failed this; the legible-bounded-entries revision fixes it.
- **Capability — holds.** The marker + evidence-phrase + weight language reads four states without analytics and is evidence-framed. Watch item: plain labels take a beat to learn; mitigated by fixed order, a constant slot, and inspect-for-evidence.
- **Ownership — holds, and strengthens over time.** The record summary and personalized capability states make the ground demonstrably *theirs*; States 2→3 the ground increasingly reflects their own evidence. Genuine strength of the model.
- **Scalability — breaks in raw A; holds in the revision.** Honestly: **Model A as first sketched does not survive 30 missions / 50 attempts** — an unbounded peripheral "ground" (all missions + all history) either crowds out the figure or drifts into dashboard/feed territory. The revision survives *only because* the default view renders a bounded slice and pushes everything unbounded one layer down. This is the load-bearing change, not a cosmetic one.
- **Accessibility — holds by construction.** Every state has a text label and a shape marker; interactions are click/tap, not hover; the gap is stated in words. Rule kept: no state is ever encoded by opacity, color, hover, or motion alone.
- **Product distinctiveness — holds only if the thesis elements are kept.** Three elements are **load-bearing, not decoration**: (1) the figure's *"because <capability gap>"* clause (gap → assignment), (2) the evidence-framed capability states (leave evidence → honest read), (3) the record-as-worked-reps (a body of practice). Strip these and it degrades to "a nicely typeset task page." Kept, it visibly expresses *do the work → leave evidence → receive an honest read → the gap generates the next assignment.*

**Structural problems found**
1. **Scale (the real one):** raw A's unbounded ground breaks at State 3 → fixed by the bounded-slice rule + summarized record + one inspection layer down.
2. **Stack risk:** two ground regions + a record could re-form the banned dashboard stack → fixed by giving each a distinct spatial channel (a five-item register band, a short titled list, a single summary line) and by bounding each.
3. **Thesis is load-bearing:** the gap/why clause and evidence framing are not garnish; without them the composition is merely editorial → they are required elements.
4. **Learnability:** the capability label language needs a fixed slot/order to become glanceable → adopted.

**Does Model A break?** Raw Model A breaks on **scalability**. It does not need to be abandoned — the fix is structural and small (bound the ground, summarize the record, push the unbounded one layer down), and with it the model survives all four states.

## The one revised composition to take into visual design

**"The Standing Assignment over a bounded practice ground."**

1. **One figure** — the recommended rep, dominant by scale/space/position/contrast, never a card; always carrying its *"because <capability gap>"* clause and the sole default Begin action.
2. **A fixed capability register (five)** — categorical marker (`○`/`●`) + evidence-phrase label (*not yet worked · shown once · shown in a few reps · shown consistently*) + reinforcing weight; gap annotated in words and mirrored in the figure. Never opacity/color/hover alone; unexplored at full legibility.
3. **A bounded practice-world region** — ~3 next-relevant missions as legible, obviously-interactive entries + `see all missions ›`; not-yet-relevant items carry a plain reason, no padlock.
4. **A record presence** — a compact summary (count + recent few, no timestamps) that grows into a felt body of practice; full history one layer down, grouped by mission/capability, never by time.
5. **Inspection layers are contained** — peeking a mission/capability/record opens a focused, dismissable surface; inspecting a mission can temporarily swap figure/ground while the recommendation stays named and one tap away.
6. **The scaling rule is part of the model** — the default Field always shows a bounded slice (5 + ~3 + 1); everything unbounded lives one layer down. This is what lets it survive 30 missions / 50 attempts with the rep still dominant.

Load-bearing and non-negotiable into styling: the gap→assignment clause, the evidence-framed capability states, and the record-as-worked-reps. These carry the product thesis; visual design dresses them, it does not remove them.

Recommended into visual design. No fonts/colors/shadows chosen. Stop.
